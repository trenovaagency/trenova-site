FAQ

QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

How much does a campaign cost?

It depends on the platforms, the number of creators and the deliverables. Send the brief and you get a clear quote with options before anything starts.

How do you pick creators?

We check audience, engagement and content quality before any pick, and you get a shortlist with each creator's real numbers and why they fit the brand.

Do you cover all of Saudi Arabia?

Yes. Our campaigns have covered Riyadh, Jeddah, Abha and more, and our creator network reaches every region of the Kingdom.

Do you work with agencies or only brands?

Both. We work with brands directly, and with agencies that need a specialist influencer arm for the Saudi market.

Can creators film content just for our own channels?

Yes, that is UGC: creators film videos for your accounts and ads without posting on their own pages, and the campaign is measured by the content delivered.
